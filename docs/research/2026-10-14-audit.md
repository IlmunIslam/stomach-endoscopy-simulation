## E. Hypotheses (settings/geometry based, no image inspection)
1. **Wet-mucosa specular/SSS may be approximated** — engine is EEVEE at 40 render samples while materials use Coat 0.35–1.0 with coat roughness 0.03–0.07 and SSS 0.18. Evidence: material values; engine setting. Confidence: low–medium.
2. **Single 69.5 W point light with 0.02 radius** likely gives small hard highlights and steep falloff; four fill/key lights are hidden. Evidence: light list; 18 mm lens on 36 mm sensor = 90° FOV (real endoscopes are typically wider, ~140°; [unverified] here). Confidence: medium.
3. **Missing textures** may leave W0/W1/colon surfaces untextured. Evidence: all 11 image paths fail `exists`; drive S: absent. Confidence: medium (not cross-checked with the missing-files tool).
4. **Dark red world, no active fill** limits indirect light contribution in EEVEE. Evidence: world colour (0.05, 0.012, 0.008); fills hidden. Confidence: low–medium.
5. **Uneven geometry density** — Stomach 275k verts with displacement and Colon 300k, but Esophagus Tube 14k and Breathing tube 160 verts, with camera clip start 0.001. Possible faceting in lower-density parts at close range. Confidence: low.
