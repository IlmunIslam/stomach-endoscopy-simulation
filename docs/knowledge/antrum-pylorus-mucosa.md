# Antrum and pylorus mucosa: what real photos show

**Use when:** judging or texturing the Antrum, pyloric ring or vein/strand work, or deciding whether a feature "belongs". Check this before adding any structure (see LESSONS 2026-10-17 "antrum vein": a large vein was invented that no photo showed; PLAYBOOK 1).

## What the references show
Looked at: `Antrum + Pylorus ring images/` (yn0432, images), `references/hyperkvasir` pylorus, `references/gastrohun` A1.
- **Colour:** uniform salmon to orange-pink, slightly paler/yellower on ridges, deeper red only around the orifice. No blue or purple.
- **Surface:** smooth, wet, finely matte-grainy under the gloss. Large soft mounds and folds, not sharp creases. Antral folds are low, mostly longitudinal, and run converging toward the pylorus (the body has the big rugae).
- **Pyloric ring:** a small, round or slit-like dark opening (about 1/10 to 1/6 of the frame), with a thick, smooth, slightly paler lip. Surrounding folds radiate into it like a puckered purse. The opening is usually off-centre and partly closed, not a clean circular tube.
- **Vessels:** essentially none visible in white light. Occasional faint, short, darker-red smudges (broad, blurry, under the surface). No branching tree, no raised strands. Literature: the antral subepithelial capillary network is a fine coil/reticular mesh and collecting venules are rarely seen, unlike the body, where regular venules show.
- **Highlights:** many small, hard, white specular dots (bubbles and mucus beads) strung along folds, plus one or two broad soft glare patches. There is no continuous white line on the mucosa.
- **Edge of view:** dark vignette and hexagonal/round mask come from the scope, not the mesh.

## Steps for our scene
1. Base colour: single warm salmon, 5 to 10% variation in low-frequency noise; slightly paler on fold crests, deeper red within ~1 ring radius of the opening.
2. Fine vessels: skip as geometry. If shown at all, use a very low-contrast, blurred, dark-red texture (see the "vessels by texture" card, still queued). Do not add a tube or object.
3. Specular: roughness about 0.25 to 0.4 with a soft broad highlight, plus a few tiny bright dots. Flag any thin continuous bright line (cf. the seam/crack and strand-glint lessons) as geometry or spec trouble, not anatomy.
4. Ring: keep a thick pale lip, opening off-centre and not perfectly round; folds converge to it.

## Pitfalls
- Treating "vein-like" as a real vein. In this region the photos show smudges, not vessels. Ask first and cite a photo.
- Making the antrum as rugose as the body.
- Perfectly circular, clean-edged pylorus (looks like a pipe; cf. lesson 2026-10-19 pyloric canal).
- Stock photos (images.jpg is watermarked, low-res) are for colour and shape only.
