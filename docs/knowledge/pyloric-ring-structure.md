# Pyloric ring / pylorus: structure for 3D model (research card, 2026-10-20)

Status: PARTIAL. Web search found little quantitative endoscopic description. Items not found are listed as such; do not treat them as facts.

## Sourced facts
- Pylorus is the end of the antrum; antrum sometimes separated from the pyloric canal by a slight groove; sphincter is a strong ring of smooth muscle. Canal wall thickness up to 8 mm in adults (3 mm infants). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pylorus (text CC BY-SA 4.0; per-image licences not stated, check Commons). Confidence: medium.
- Radiographic/physiology study: the "pyloric ring" is not a separate structure but the aboral end of a muscular tube ("several centimeters" long). Peristaltic waves stop at the oral end and trigger a concentric squeeze of the whole cylinder including the ring; at rest the cylinder including the ring is open. https://scholar.sun.ac.za/items/608ad3e4-05a3-48cc-beeb-b8ca4b24cf82 (Keet 1982; no licence stated, cite only). Confidence: medium (abstract only).
- A Japanese endoscopy paper also calls the ring a short "pyloric tube", with the mucosal junction on its duodenal side (reported secondhand via search summary). https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/browse/gee1973a/list/-char/en Confidence: low.
- WebPath has normal endoscopic images: antrum extending to pylorus right of centre, duodenal bulb beyond. Text gives no morphology; I did not view the pixels. https://webpath.med.utah.edu/GIHTML/GI195.html (no licence stated; reference only, do not copy). Confidence: high for existence, none for detail.
- SAGES image page "The Pylorus" (pylorus kept centred; sometimes seen opening and closing) could not be fetched (HTTP 403); only a search snippet. https://sages.org/image-library/the-pylorus Confidence: low.
- Pseudopylorus (antral mucosa ring) vs true pylorus (duodenal mucosa) - Endoscopy 1971. https://www.thieme-connect.de/products/ejournals/pdf/10.1055/s-0028-1098121.pdf Confidence: medium.

- A "2 cm" canal length for humans (not supported; a 3-5 cm "cylinder" figure exists in Keet but includes the antral terminal segment).

## Opinion / common-knowledge, unsourced here [unverified, low-medium confidence]
- Closed pylorus: rosette/star of converging antral folds to a small, often slightly off-centre dimple; open: round to oval aperture.
- Lip colour similar to antral mucosa (pink-orange); duodenal bulb paler, velvety.
- Modelling suggestion: build as a short muscular tube (not a thin ring) with a soft torus cushion, folds converging radially, aperture driven by a shape key/radius control.
